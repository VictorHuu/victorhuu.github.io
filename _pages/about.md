Hi! I am **Yuntao Hu**, a graduate student at **Tongji University**. My research interests lie at the intersection of <mark class="ox-mark" markdown="span">**AI for Science**</mark> and <mark class="ox-mark" markdown="span">**Agentic Reinforcement Learning**</mark>, with a particular focus on applying modern machine learning methods to scientific and real-world decision-making problems.

Currently, I am interested in **foundation models for biology**, especially representation learning for **single-cell transcriptomics**, as well as **reinforcement learning and autonomous agents for financial decision-making**.
